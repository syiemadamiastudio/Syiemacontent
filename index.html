About

Syiema Content Studio is a private content workflow tool used by its owner to prepare and upload video content to the owner's TikTok account as drafts for review, editing and posting.

The service is intended for the owner and authorized test users only.

TikTok integration

The tool may use TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API permissions explicitly authorized by the TikTok account owner.
