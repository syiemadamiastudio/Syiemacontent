Effective date: 2 September 2026

Purpose

This policy explains how Syiema Content Studio handles information when the owner or an authorized test user connects a TikTok account.

Information processed

The service may process basic TikTok account information, authorization tokens required for the connection, and video files or metadata supplied for draft upload.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized TikTok account, operate the content workflow, upload videos as drafts, troubleshoot the integration and maintain the service.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be transmitted to TikTok and service providers required to operate the workflow only as necessary to provide the service.

Storage and security

Authentication credentials and workflow data may be stored or processed only as necessary for the integration, with access limited to authorized systems.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate or troubleshoot the private workflow.

User control

An authorized user may revoke TikTok access through TikTok account settings or disconnect the integration.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the workflow, connected services or platform requirements change.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the owner of Syiema Content Studio through the contact details associated with the application.
